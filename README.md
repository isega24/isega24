# Iván Sevillano-García

**Postdoctoral researcher** at the [Andalusian Research Institute in Data Science and Computational Intelligence (DaSCI)](https://dasci.es) and the Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, University of Granada.

I work on making machine learning models explainable and reliable. My research runs along four lines:

- **Evaluating explanations** — metrics and frameworks that tell whether a local explanation is robust, concise and actionable, instead of merely plausible.
- **Explanations as feedback** — regularizations that use explanations to improve the model itself, removing the need for human-labelled explanations.
- **Out-of-distribution detection** — statistical tests that flag OOD inputs and then explain why they were flagged.
- **XAI for LLM-based agents** — how to evaluate and explain the reasoning of agentic systems.

PhD in Information and Communication Technologies, University of Granada (2025), advised by Francisco Herrera and Julián Luengo.
Thesis: *Inteligencia Artificial Explicable: Métodos para generación, evaluación y corrección de explicaciones* — [handle](https://hdl.handle.net/10481/112935).
